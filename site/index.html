egmp-aa-ev-feat

Contribute EV logs

Help Google Maps predict range better on Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, EV6, EV9 and GV60 head units.

What these logs are

With egmp-aa-ev-feat installed, the head unit sends Google Maps an energy model of the car: how much energy it uses at a given speed and temperature, and how fast it charges. That model is fitted from only a few data points, mostly from one Ioniq 5. A log records, every few seconds whenever the head unit is on (driving, charging or parked, with or without Android Auto), the battery level and energy, speed, temperatures, climate and charging state, and the model values sent to Maps, so we can compare what Maps predicted with what the car really used, and fit the model for more cars, climates and battery ages.

What is recorded

One CSV file each time the head unit is on. Only the fields below exist in the format; this page and tools/evlog.py remove anything else before you share a file.

    The car is identified only by its head unit software version and the first part of the VIN (characters 1–8 and 10: maker, model, body, motor and model year), which is the same for every car of that model and year.

    How to collect logs

    1. On a FAT32 or exFAT USB drive, create an empty folder named egmp-aa-ev-logs.
    2. Plug the drive into the car's USB data port (the one Android Auto uses) with the car on.
    3. The head unit copies new logs into that folder by itself. Leave the drive in for a minute, then take it out.

    Logging whenever the head unit is on (including while charging, without Android Auto) comes with the next release; v0.2.0 logs during Android Auto sessions. Cold drives, highway trips and fast-charging sessions are the most useful.

    Check and clean your logs

    Files are read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded until you choose to attach them to a GitHub issue yourself.

    No logs yet? Try the sample log.

    Submit on GitHub

    Submitting creates a public GitHub issue. The issue form opens with your car, dates and durations filled in. Download the cleaned files first, then drag them into the issue's Log files box. Anyone can see and download attached files.
